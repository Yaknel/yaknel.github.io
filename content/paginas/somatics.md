## Yoga Movement & Breathing

I’ve been practising Ashtanga Yoga, Meditation and Movement for over 15 years.

I focus in the biodynamics of the body, anatomy and exploring breathing, using ourselves, our experiences & our breath as our teachers. 

Everyone can practise yoga. It doesn’t matter what age, shape or size you are. What unfolds on your mat offers insight into the direction of your daily life.

Nothing is stable : focus on your breath : practice : each day
